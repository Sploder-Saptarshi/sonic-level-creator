Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Sonic ReLvl! Create a FREE account to build your own Sonic the Hedgehog levels, share them with the community, and play levels made by other members.

This website is an unofficial, non-profit revival of Mediatonic & SEGA's "PlaySEGA" service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mediatonic or SEGA.
 
 
 
Start a new level
Open the Level Creator, give your level a name and pick a map size. You can come back to it any time from My Levels.
Build it
Paint terrain from the Tiles tab, then drag in objects, rings and enemies from their tabs. Every level needs exactly one Sonic Spawn and one Sign Post from the Essentials tab: without both, a level can be neither saved nor tested. Each tile area holds up to 7 objects, and each object needs a placed tile.
Test, save and share
Press Test Level to play your level right inside the editor. Save keeps it in My Levels. Share publishes it permanently so it appears in All Levels for everyone.
 
Playing levels
Browse All Levels to play levels shared by the community. In the editor, press Stop testing to leave test mode and keep building.
 
Managing your levels
My Levels lists everything you have saved. Open a level to keep editing it, Rename it, or Delete it. A level stays unpublished until you share it; published levels carry a Published label and cannot be taken back.
