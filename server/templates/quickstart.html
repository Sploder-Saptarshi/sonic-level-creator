Find out more about Guest passes and becoming a Member or VIP.
 
 
 
What are PlaySEGA Rings?
Rings are the PlaySEGA currency that you can earn while playing games on the site. Any member can trade rings to enter competitions, buy Escapes and luxury items to pimp your pad and accessories to customize your Avatar.
How do I collect rings?
Registered members will receive 5,000 free rings to get you started on sign up while VIP's receive 5,000 free rings and double points for every single ring you collect. The more games you play, the more rings you can earn.
